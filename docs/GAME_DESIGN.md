# Game design

The first session moves from tree survival to a cobblestone generator and starter farm. Coins supplement play rather than replace it: unlocks require verified challenges or island level as well as money.

Four arcs—survival, economy, industry, and endgame—support both cooperation and friendly competition. Personal wallets prevent team disputes; BentoBox owns shared island progress. Daily challenges are bonuses, never time gates.

Generator tiers remain disabled in the shop until a supported addon/API is runtime-verified. No purchase may claim an upgrade that does not change gameplay.
